## Stages defined

| Stage | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Lead** | Interested or warm, no discovery call yet |
| **Discovery** | Call scheduled or completed; scoping the work |
| **Proposal** | Proposal sent, awaiting decision |
| **Engaged** | Contract signed, work in progress |
| **Delivered** | Work shipped, invoice pending |
| **Invoiced** | Invoice sent, payment pending |
| **Paid** | Engagement closed, money in |
| **Lost** | Declined or went elsewhere |
| **Pipeline** | Long-term warm channel, no specific work surfaced |

---

## What this tracker is NOT

- **Hours tracking** — use Toggl / Harvest / Clockify for billable time
- **Detailed deliverables** — per-engagement details live in `interview-prep/` (call notes) or a future `proposals/` folder (formal proposals)
- **Invoicing / accounting** — use accounting software (FreshBooks, Wave, etc.)
- **Client communication archive** — keep call notes and email threads separately

This file is just the **dashboard**: what's in flight, what's next, what's stuck.

## Operating notes

- **Sequencing rule:** consulting work fits AROUND active FT job search. Default capacity: 10–20 hr/week. Don't promise FT-equivalent hours on any single engagement.
- **Honesty disclosure:** when recommending tools owned by people in the network (e.g., Kawsty / Lawrence), disclose the relationship in writing in the proposal. Never silent.
- **Referral hygiene:** when an engagement closes (won OR lost), send the referrer (BONG, Justin, etc.) a thanks-for-the-intro note. Keeps the lane warm.
- **Disqualification is fine:** if an engagement isn't a good fit, walk away cleanly with a "not the right fit, here's who might be" message. Better than a bad engagement.
