## Live-coding posture

- **Talk while coding.** "I'm going to use querySelectorAll, then iterate and toggle a class…" Narration is half the eval.
- **Ask clarifying questions.** "Should this handle empty inputs?" "Are we styling or just toggling state?" Senior-IC move.
- **Trace through with one example informally.** Don't just write — verify.
- **Pauses are normal.** Don't fill silence with softeners. Filling silence with "um, I think maybe…" is exactly what the self-debrief flagged.

**If Remix-flavored prompt:** lead confidently. **If vanilla-React-flavored:** take a beat, narrate reasoning, ask whether server-side data-loading patterns are in scope.
