## What this call is (and isn't)

This is a **client evaluation**, not a job interview. You're assessing *her* as much as she's assessing *you*. The goal is a mutual read: is there a real problem, is there a real budget, is the shape of the work a fit for how you work — and is she someone you want as a client.

Outcome to aim for by 2:30pm: enough information to write a short written proposal afterward (or to politely pass). You don't need to commit to scope or price on the call.

**Reminder before the call:** review your actual BONG post so you remember what she's responding to. Her first line references it — missing the context reads as "I post a lot and don't track replies."

Information you need to write a proposal (or walk away cleanly):

1. **Current stack.** POS (Toast/Square/Clover)? Website platform? Online ordering (ChowNow/Square/Shopify/custom)? Email/marketing tools? What does "digital" currently look like for both concepts?

5. **Timeline.** Festival season, lunch-rush cycles, any specific event she's targeting.
6. **Decision style.** Does she decide alone, or with a partner/accountant/investor?
7. **What "success" looks like.** A number she wants to move, a time she wants to save, a stress she wants to offload?
