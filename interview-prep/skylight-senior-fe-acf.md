## Plain-English acronym glossary

- **GSA** — General Services Administration. Federal "back-office" agency that handles buildings, procurement, and shared tech services for the rest of government.
- **18F** — A software dev team built *inside* GSA in March 2014 after the Healthcare.gov launch disaster. Brought Silicon Valley practices to federal agencies as an internal consultancy. Worked on Cloud.gov, Login.gov, VA.gov. Reduced/dissolved in waves — most notably during the first Trump admin and again under DOGE in early 2025. Much of the alumni base is now scattered across private civic-tech consultancies.
- **ACF** — Administration for Children and Families. Operating division inside HHS. Runs/funds child welfare, foster care, refugee resettlement, Head Start, child support enforcement, TANF, tribal family services. ~$60B annual budget.
- **HHS** — Department of Health and Human Services. ACF is a sub-agency.
- **IDIQ** — Indefinite Delivery, Indefinite Quantity. A multi-year federal contract vehicle where the government pre-commits a ceiling of work (here $88M) to a vendor (here Skylight is sole vendor — "single-award"). Call-orders are issued under the IDIQ over the period. Much more stable than a single project award.
- **VIBES** — "Value-driven, Iterate, Build, Evolve, Scale." The name ACF gave this IDIQ to signal it's agile / iterative rather than big-bang waterfall.
- **ATO** — Authority to Operate. The federal compliance gate every new IT system passes before handling federal data. Historically takes 6–18 months of paperwork.
- **SBIR** — Small Business Innovation Research. Federal grant program that funds R&D with small companies. Phase III = commercialization stage.
- **HCD** — Human-Centered Design. Civic-tech shorthand for design research + iteration + stakeholder interviews before writing code.
- **Section 508 / WCAG** — Federal accessibility standards. WCAG 2.1 AA is the typical target.
